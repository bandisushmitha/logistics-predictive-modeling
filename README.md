# Logistics Delivery Optimization and Demand Forecasting

## Week 1 - Strategic Planning and Data Exploration

### Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing logistics and delivery data to identify factors affecting delivery performance, transportation cost, fuel cost, and on-time delivery.

Week 1 focuses on understanding the logistics problem, exploring the dataset, calculating key performance indicators (KPIs), and identifying important relationships between logistics variables.

### Objectives

- Understand logistics delivery performance
- Explore shipment and transportation data
- Identify missing and duplicate records
- Calculate important logistics KPIs
- Analyze regional performance
- Analyze the impact of traffic conditions
- Compare vehicle performance
- Identify relationships between numerical variables
- Create visualizations for better understanding

### Dataset

The dataset contains 600 logistics shipment records with 11 attributes:

- Date
- Region
- Vehicle Type
- Traffic
- Category
- Shipment Volume
- Distance (km)
- Fuel Cost
- Delivery Time (hours)
- Transport Cost
- On-Time Delivery

### Week 1 Outcome

The exploratory analysis provides an initial understanding of logistics operations and identifies important factors that can influence delivery performance.

The findings from Week 1 will be used as the foundation for data cleaning and preprocessing in Week 2, followed by advanced analysis, predictive modeling, and optimization in later stages.
